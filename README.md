### FAQs (Full)

**Booking & Availability**

* **Q:** How do I rent a car with AAZ Car Rental?
  **A:** Call or WhatsApp our team — we will respond and get your car delivered under 60 minutes across Dubai.

* **Q:** Can I drive the rental car outside Dubai?
  **A:** At AAZ you can drive cars within UAE territory.

* **Q:** What is the cost of renting a car in Dubai?
  **A:** Prices vary by car type, rental duration, and delivery location. We offer affordable options — cars can start from as low as AED 80/day.

* **Q:** What documents do I need as a tourist?
  **A:** Visit visa, passport, driving license from home country, and depending on nationality an International Driving Permit (IDP). AAZ also assists with getting the IDP to simplify the process.

* **Q:** Do I need a deposit?
  **A:** Yes — a refundable deposit is required for safety; it’s returned one month after the car is returned.

* **Q:** Can I rent without a credit card?
  **A:** Yes — we accept cash, debit card, and online bank transfers.

**Documentation**

* **Residents:** Emirates ID and Driver’s License.
* **Tourists:** Visit visa, passport, home country driving license, and possibly an IDP (we assist with IDP).

**Fleet & Services**

* **Q:** What types of cars are available?
  **A:** Economy, family SUVs, group cars, luxury cars, sports cars, and convertibles with GPS and child car seat available without additional charges.

* **Q:** Do you provide airport and hotel delivery?
  **A:** Yes — we offer airport and hotel pickup/drop-off across Dubai.

* **Q:** Do you offer long-term or monthly rentals?
  **A:** Yes — ideal for residents or visitors staying for extended periods.

**Fuel & Parking**

* **Q:** Do I need to return the car with a full tank?
  **A:** Yes — full-to-full fuel policy.

* **Q:** How much does fuel cost in Dubai?
  **A:** Fuel typically ranges from AED 2.30–2.70 per liter (government regulated).

* **Q:** Is parking easily available?
  **A:** Mall and hotel parking is usually free; for other locations RTA parking fees may apply.

**Safety & Rules**

* **Q:** What are the speed limits in Dubai?
  **A:** Highways 100–120 km/h, city areas 60–80 km/h, residential zones 40–60 km/h.

* **Q:** Is insurance included?
  **A:** Yes — all cars have comprehensive insurance covering accidents and breakdowns.
